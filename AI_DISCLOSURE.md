## 2. AI models inside the submitted system

- **CLIP ViT-B/16** (OpenAI, MIT licence): pretrained and used zero-shot, with no fine-tuning, for
  the video label, highlight relevance and noun recognition.
- **Verb prior:** P(verb | noun) counted from the EPIC-KITCHENS-100 **train** annotations
  (CC BY-NC 4.0). The EPIC validation annotations, which are the test set, and the FPVSum
  annotations were never used, for training or for tuning.
- No generative AI produces the outputs. Every output JSON is written by `run.sh` from the video file,
  with no hardcoded video IDs, no ground truth, and no labels read from file names.

## 3. How the outputs were produced

All outputs were generated by `run.sh` (through `code/scripts/stream_run.py`) on a Google Colab T4 GPU.
Before the deadline, 86 of the 185 scored videos were processed (all 47 FPVSum and 39 of 138
EPIC-KITCHENS-100). The remaining videos have no output file. All 86 files pass `validate.py`.
